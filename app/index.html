Roadmap to the platinum

About 200 hours, difficulty 4/10, one playthrough, no online trophies. Cheats disable trophies and saving — leave them off. Keep a save at the start and end of each chapter (15 manual slots); there is no mission replay.

⚠ The one missable trophy: Positive Mental Attitude — one meditation only happens in Chapter 1, at Jason’s house. After the mission Down to Earth, talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning, follow her to the balcony and choose Accept. It is still unclear whether it is required for the trophy, so do it while you can.
